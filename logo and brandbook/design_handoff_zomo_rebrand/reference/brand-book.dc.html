01 · SYMBOL

The route z

The symbol is a lowercase z drawn as one trip. It starts at an attached pickup dot, runs across, cuts back and ends at a separate drop-off dot. It reads as a letter at 16px and as a route at billboard size.

02 · WORDMARK

zomo, drawn not typed

Custom monoline lettering on a 100u x-height with a 22u stroke. Both o's are true circles of the same size, so z-o-m-o reads as two stops with the m between them. The z carries the pickup dot from the symbol.

ზომო
Georgian lockup · typeset draft in IBM Plex Sans Georgian Bold

Use the Georgian lockup where the interface or the audience is Georgian-first, such as street posters, the Georgian app store listing and printed receipts. Use the Latin wordmark everywhere else, including the app icon and the domain.

The Georgian letters still need custom drawing to match the 22u stroke and the circular o (ო). Have a native-script designer review that version before it is used in production.

05 · TYPOGRAPHY

IBM Plex, Latin and Georgian

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Georgian share proportions, so bilingual screens keep one rhythm. Never use the typeface to set the wordmark.

07 · FLEET LIVERY

Vans and box trucks

White vans carry the route line: an orange stripe with a pickup dot and a drop-off dot that runs the length of the body. Box trucks are painted orange with the white lockup, so they can be read from across a street. The cab always carries the symbol.

Small van · side. Lockup at 100u on the rear panel; zomo.ge in deep orange on the front door.
Small van · rear. The symbol and the wordmark sit on separate doors and never cross the seam.
Box truck · 3.5t. Full orange box with the white lockup; the symbol is repeated in orange on the cab door.

09 · MISUSE

Don't

✕ Stretch or squash
✕ Rotate or tilt
✕ Recolour outside the palette
✕ Add shadows, gradients or 3D
✕ Orange on orange or busy photos
✕ Rearrange or respace the lockup
